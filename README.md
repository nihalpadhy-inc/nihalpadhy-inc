## <img src="./assets/heading-about.svg" alt="About" height="32">

I come from finance and business analytics. At **[Praxys Solutions](https://praxyssolutions.com/)** I start by learning how a business actually runs. Then I build the AI agents, data pipelines, and Power BI reporting that let it qualify and follow up with leads, automate recurring work, and see what is driving results.
